# VAT for EU customers

_Section: Billing_ · _Last updated: 2025-12-02_

Paperkite calculates VAT on each invoice line, including prorated lines.

- The rate is that of the customer's billing country, at the invoice date.
- VAT is applied to the net, prorated amount.
- Business customers with a valid VAT ID (checked against VIES) are invoiced at 0% under the reverse charge.
- One Stop Shop (OSS) reporting is supported.
