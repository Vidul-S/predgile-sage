# Invoices: numbering, languages and payment terms

_Section: Billing_ · _Last updated: 2026-01-12_

Paperkite invoices can be customised to match your accounting.

- Set a custom prefix and the next invoice number under **Settings > Invoices**.
- Invoice PDFs are available in 14 languages.
- Payment terms can be Net 7, Net 14, Net 30 or Net 60.
- Automatic reminders are sent 3 days before the due date and 1 and 7 days after.
