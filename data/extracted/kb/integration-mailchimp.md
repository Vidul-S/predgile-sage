# Paperkite for Mailchimp

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-05-15_

The Mailchimp integration connects Paperkite to your marketing workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 1.8.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Starter.
- Data sync: hourly.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select Mailchimp and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
