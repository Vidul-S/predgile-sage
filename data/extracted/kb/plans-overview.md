# Plans overview

_Section: Account_ · _Last updated: 2025-03-04_

Paperkite has four plans: Starter, Pro, Scale and Enterprise.

- Starter: 2 seats, 100 requests per minute.
- Pro: 10 seats, 1,000 requests per minute, instant payouts.
- Scale: unlimited seats, 5,000 requests per minute, instant payouts, SSO.
- Enterprise: custom limits and a dedicated manager.

On 1 March 2025 Basic was renamed Starter and Business was renamed Scale.
