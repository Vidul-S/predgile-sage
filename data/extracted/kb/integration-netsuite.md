# Paperkite for NetSuite

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-10-15_

The NetSuite integration connects Paperkite to your accounting workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 2.0.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Scale.
- Data sync: daily.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select NetSuite and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
