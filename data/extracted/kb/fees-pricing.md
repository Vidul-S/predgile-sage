# Pricing and fees

_Section: Billing_ · _Last updated: 2026-04-22_

## Plans

- Starter: €0 per month.
- Pro: €49 per month.
- Scale: €299 per month.
- Enterprise: custom pricing.

## Card payments (per successful charge)

- Starter: 2.4% + €0.25.
- Pro: 1.9% + €0.20.
- Scale: 1.5% + €0.15.

## Other fees

- SEPA Direct Debit: 0.8%, capped at €5.00.
- Instant payouts: 1.5%, minimum €0.50.
- Disputes: €15 per dispute, returned if you win.
- Currency conversion: 1.0%.
- There are no setup fees.
