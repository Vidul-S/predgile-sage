# Paperkite for WooCommerce

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-11-15_

The WooCommerce integration connects Paperkite to your e-commerce workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 3.8.
- Subscriptions: supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Starter.
- Data sync: real time.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select WooCommerce and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
