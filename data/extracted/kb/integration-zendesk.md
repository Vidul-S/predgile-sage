# Paperkite for Zendesk

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-03-15_

The Zendesk integration connects Paperkite to your support workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 3.0.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Pro.
- Data sync: real time.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select Zendesk and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
