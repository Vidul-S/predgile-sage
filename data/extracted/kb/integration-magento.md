# Paperkite for Magento

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-12-15_

The Magento integration connects Paperkite to your e-commerce workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 2.4.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Pro.
- Data sync: every 15 minutes.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select Magento and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
