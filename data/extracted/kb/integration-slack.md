# Paperkite for Slack

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-02-15_

The Slack integration connects Paperkite to your notifications workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 2.0.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Starter.
- Data sync: real time.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select Slack and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
