# Paperkite for FreshBooks

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-11-15_

The FreshBooks integration connects Paperkite to your accounting workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 1.1.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Starter.
- Data sync: daily.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select FreshBooks and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
