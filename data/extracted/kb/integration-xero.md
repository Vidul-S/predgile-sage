# Paperkite for Xero

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-09-15_

The Xero integration connects Paperkite to your accounting workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 4.4.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Pro.
- Data sync: daily.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select Xero and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
