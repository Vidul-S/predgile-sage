# Paperkite for QuickBooks

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-08-15_

The QuickBooks integration connects Paperkite to your accounting workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 5.1.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Pro.
- Data sync: daily.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select QuickBooks and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
