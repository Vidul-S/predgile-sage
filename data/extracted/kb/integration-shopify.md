# Paperkite for Shopify

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-10-15_

The Shopify integration connects Paperkite to your e-commerce workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 4.2.
- Subscriptions: supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Starter.
- Data sync: real time.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select Shopify and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
