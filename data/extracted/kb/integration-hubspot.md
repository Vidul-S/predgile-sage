# Paperkite for HubSpot

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-06-15_

The HubSpot integration connects Paperkite to your CRM workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 3.3.
- Subscriptions: supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Pro.
- Data sync: every 5 minutes.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select HubSpot and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
