# Glossary of terms

_Section: Help Center_ · _Last updated: 2025-09-18_

Everything you need to know about glossary of terms.

- **Payment**: one attempt to collect money.
- **Payout**: a transfer of your balance to your bank.
- **Dispute**: a challenge raised by a cardholder.
- **Idempotency key**: a value that makes a request safe to retry.
