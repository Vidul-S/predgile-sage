# Paperkite for Pipedrive

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-07-15_

The Pipedrive integration connects Paperkite to your CRM workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 1.4.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Pro.
- Data sync: hourly.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select Pipedrive and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
