# Paperkite for PrestaShop

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-01-15_

The PrestaShop integration connects Paperkite to your e-commerce workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 1.7.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Starter.
- Data sync: real time.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select PrestaShop and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
