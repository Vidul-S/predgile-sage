# Paperkite for BigCommerce

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-02-15_

The BigCommerce integration connects Paperkite to your e-commerce workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 2.1.
- Subscriptions: supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Pro.
- Data sync: real time.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select BigCommerce and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
