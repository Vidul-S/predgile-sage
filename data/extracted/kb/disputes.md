# Handling disputes

_Section: Payments_ · _Last updated: 2025-08-12_

A dispute opens when a cardholder challenges a payment with their bank.

- You have 7 days from the dispute notification to submit evidence.
- Each dispute carries a €15 fee, returned if you win.
- Useful evidence: proof of delivery, customer communication, signed terms.
- Submit evidence under **Payments > Disputes**.
