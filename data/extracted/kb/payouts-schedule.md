# Payout schedule

_Section: Payments_ · _Last updated: 2026-04-03_

Standard payouts leave your balance on a fixed schedule that depends on your plan.

- Starter: 5 business days.
- Pro: 2 business days.
- Scale: 1 business day (next business day).

For accounts settling in countries outside the EU, the UK and the US, add 2 business days to each schedule. The daily cut-off is 17:00 UTC.
