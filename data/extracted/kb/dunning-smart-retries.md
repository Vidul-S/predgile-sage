# Smart Retries for failed subscription payments

_Section: Billing_ · _Last updated: 2025-06-24_

When a subscription payment fails, Smart Retries tries again at the best time for the card.

- Paperkite retries 4 times over 14 days.
- Customers receive emails on days 1, 5 and 12.
- After the final failure the subscription is marked unpaid or canceled, as you configure.
