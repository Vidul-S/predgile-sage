# Attaching files to invoices

_Section: Billing_ · _Last updated: 2025-09-20_

Add supporting files to an invoice before you send it.

- Each attachment can be up to 10 MB.
- You can attach up to 5 files to one invoice.
- Accepted formats are PDF, PNG, JPG and CSV.
- Attachments are delivered as links in the invoice email and on the hosted invoice page.
