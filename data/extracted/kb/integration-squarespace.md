# Paperkite for Squarespace

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-04-15_

The Squarespace integration connects Paperkite to your website workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 1.2.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Starter.
- Data sync: hourly.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select Squarespace and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
