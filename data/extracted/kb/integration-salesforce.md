# Paperkite for Salesforce

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-05-15_

The Salesforce integration connects Paperkite to your CRM workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 6.0.
- Subscriptions: supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Scale.
- Data sync: every 5 minutes.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select Salesforce and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
