# Supported payment methods

_Section: Payments_ · _Last updated: 2026-03-15_

Paperkite supports these payment methods. Availability depends on the customer's country; see the country pages.

- Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Cartes Bancaires)
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- SEPA Direct Debit
- ACH Debit
- iDEAL, Bancontact, BLIK, EPS, Multibanco, TWINT
