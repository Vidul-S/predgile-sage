# Paperkite for Wix

_Section: Integrations_ · _Last updated: 2025-03-15_

The Wix integration connects Paperkite to your website workflow.

- Minimum plugin version: 1.5.
- Subscriptions: not supported.
- Minimum Paperkite plan: Starter.
- Data sync: every 15 minutes.

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Select Wix and sign in.
3. Choose the data to sync and save.
